# Disposition of Gemini High review

Two validated headless High consultations were used: design and results. Both returned SUCCESS with nonempty text and no tools. Reported totals: 16,745 + 18,864 = 35,609 tokens, separate from DeepSeek experiment usage. These are AI suggestions, not author adjudication.

Accepted design improvements were frozen before inference: eliminate redundant kind enum, use explicit claim_ids/nf_reason, preserve mixed-content declaration, keep only real discrete source references, stably sort without rewriting claims, make joint semantic/structural success primary, audit raw omissions separately, and report per-answer repeated success. A reviewer also ensured both ledger arms have occurrence IDs; echo is the sole intended difference between them. Task-semantic instructions were copied from legacy rather than strengthened with observed failures.

Accepted result conclusions: retain all-attempt denominators; do not confuse recovered coverage failures with semantic omission fixes; disclose transport outcomes and unavailable usage; distinguish the known 37-pair cost comparison from the unknown 40-call echo total; do not integrate this candidate because cost and semantic non-regression gates fail. Preserve the durable run tooling and bounded exploratory result.

Claims in the returned advice that go beyond the evidence are NOT adopted:

- Copy text acting as a semantic decoding anchor is a plausible hypothesis, not an established causal mechanism. The shared-valid differences on this small batch do not measure internal anchoring or rule out stochastic differences.
- 77 received ledger responses passed structural checking in this run. That does not prove permanent elimination of schema/coverage failures, and the new coarser contract is not equivalent to the old one.
- A possible relationship between output length and three transport interruptions is untested; all three occurred in position zero. No network mechanism was diagnosed.
- Larger attached unit fragments can retain a source qualifier that a shorter legacy quote omits. They do not automatically fix independent claim_text pronouns. Standalone target counts, source support and completion must not be conflated.
- Coverage-completion gains remain real availability observations under the changed protocol. The raw diagnostic does not erase those gains; it limits claims about semantic improvement. Common-valid results supplement, not replace, the fixed all-attempt comparison.

Two possible follow-up directions remain hypotheses and were NOT implemented or tested in this turn:

1. A compact ledger could remove repeated JSON fields/reason prose while still requiring the model to explicitly distinguish factual, non-factual and mixed units. Gemini's suggested disjoint `claim-unit union` versus `unclaimed_unit_ids` does not preserve the present mixed-unit declaration, and “unclaimed” must never be silently treated as non-factual. A future compact protocol must specify these semantics before evaluation rather than claim its contract is unchanged.
2. A short exact source anchor might preserve useful context while avoiding full echo. It must be tested separately from output-order/first-classification changes; adding both at once is not a single-variable ablation. A fixed 2–5 English-word rule is not appropriate as a universal Chinese/English invariant. Literal substring validation alone still cannot certify modality or factuality.

No follow-up will be tuned on these 20 exposed answers or use their text, IDs, labels or characteristic phrases as inference rules. Any later experiment needs new material, preregistration and unchanged all-attempt/semantic/cost accounting.
