## 原回答

To find someone in France, one option is to visit the Pages Jaunes website (https://www.pagesjaunes.fr/pb.cgi?lang=en) and use the last name of the person you are searching for as the search term in the Nom field. You can also include any additional information such as the first name, address, or phone number if available. If the person has not been seen for over 10 or 20 years, it may be helpful to try contacting the town hall in their city of birth, particularly if they were born there. Another option is to post an MC (missed connection) on Craigslist or attempt to locate the person through the French white pages.
