## 原回答

To plant a sprouted potato, cut it into chunks with two or three eyes each. Place the potato chunks on a tray or cookie sheet and let them dry out overnight. Dig a trench about 4 inches wide and 6 to 8 inches deep in a prepared planting area. Place the sprouted or eye side of the potato chunks facing up, separating each chunk by about 6 to 8 inches. Cover the potatoes with a layer of compost and then replace the dirt on top. Water the potatoes thoroughly and continue watering regularly when the soil is dry to the touch. Add a 4-inch layer of mulch around the plants to keep the soil cool and maintain moisture.
